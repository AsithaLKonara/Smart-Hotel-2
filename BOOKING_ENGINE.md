# SmartHotel OS — Booking Engine Specification

## 1. Overview
The SmartHotel OS Booking Engine is a high-concurrency reservation system that guarantees inventory integrity across multiple sales channels. It manages the lifecycle of a guest's stay from initial search to final checkout.

---

## 2. Booking Lifecycle (State Machine)
Reservations follow a strict progression enforced by the `BookingStatus` enum:

- `PENDING`: Initial state during payment processing.
- `CONFIRMED`: Reservation secured with payment/guarantee.
- `CHECKED_IN`: Guest has arrived; room status changed to `OCCUPIED`.
- `CHECKED_OUT`: Guest has departed; room status changed to `CLEANING`.
- `CANCELLED`: Reservation voided; inventory released.

---

## 3. Concurrency & Race Condition Prevention
The system employs a **Multi-Layer Locking Strategy** to prevent double-bookings:

1. **Distributed Lock (Redis)**: When a guest enters the final booking step, a 10-minute lease is acquired for the `roomId` and `dates`.
2. **Atomic Inventory Update (Prisma)**: The final write to the `Booking` and `Room` models is wrapped in a transaction that checks the `Room.version` and `lockExpiresAt` fields.
3. **Idempotency**: All booking requests must include an `idempotency-key` (usually generated by the client). Re-submitting the same key returns the cached response instead of creating a new booking.

---

## 4. Room Lifecycle Management
Room statuses are managed by the `RoomLifecycleEngine` (FSM).

### Key Transitions
- **Check-In**: `RESERVED` -> `CHECKED_IN` (Room becomes `OCCUPIED`).
- **Check-Out**: `CHECKED_IN` -> `HOUSEKEEPING_PENDING` (Room becomes `DIRTY`).
- **Cleaning Complete**: `HOUSEKEEPING_PENDING` -> `AVAILABLE` (Room becomes `READY_FOR_SALE`).

---

## 5. Overlap Prevention Logic
The engine calculates availability by intersecting requested date ranges with existing `CONFIRMED` and `CHECKED_IN` bookings:

$$\text{Conflict} = (\text{Start}_A < \text{End}_B) \land (\text{End}_A > \text{Start}_B)$$

Any overlap results in an immediate rejection of the booking request to protect inventory.

---

## 6. OTA Synchronization
- **Outbound**: Local inventory changes are pushed to Booking.com/Agoda via the `pushAvailabilityToOTA` service.
- **Inbound**: Webhooks from OTAs are processed by idempotent listeners that secure the room immediately in the local database.
- **Reconciliation**: A background worker audits parity between the local DB and external channels every 10 minutes.
