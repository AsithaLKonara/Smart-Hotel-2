# PMS-002 - Double-Entry Ledger Bypass (Night Audit)

## Severity

P0 - Critical

---

## Category

PMS

---

## Description

Night Audit cron bypasses `FinancialEngine` and inserts raw `FolioLineItem` with hardcoded 10% tax, breaking general ledger sync and calculating wrong taxes.

---

## Affected Files

- app/api/cron/night-audit/roll-forward/route.ts

---

## Root Cause

Cron job implemented independently from core financial logic.

---

## Production Impact

- General Ledger discrepancies. Immediate financial audit failures.

---

## How to Reproduce

Run night audit. Compare taxes on automated charges vs manual charges.

---

## Fix Guide

Step 1: Refactor Night Audit to execute `FinancialEngine.postCharge`.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Night Audit charges generate correct GL entries and 25% tax.

---

## Regression Tests

- Night audit simulation tests.

---

Sprint 1
