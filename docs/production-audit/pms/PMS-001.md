# PMS-001 - Room Assignment vs. Stay Desynchronization

## Severity

P0 - Critical

---

## Category

PMS

---

## Description

Check-in creates `Stay` for new room but ignores old `RoomAssignment`. Checkout uses `RoomAssignment`, marking wrong rooms DIRTY and keeping physical rooms permanently OCCUPIED.

---

## Affected Files

- lib/services/StayService.ts
app/api/admin/bookings/[id]/checkout/route.ts

---

## Root Cause

Split-brain logic between Check-in and Checkout processes.

---

## Production Impact

- Inventory permanently locked. Housekeeping cleaning wrong rooms.

---

## How to Reproduce

Move guest to new room at check-in. Process checkout.

---

## Fix Guide

Step 1: Ensure Check-in process deletes/updates `RoomAssignment` to match the `Stay`.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Correct room is marked DIRTY upon checkout.

---

## Regression Tests

- E2E PMS lifecycle tests with room moves.

---

Sprint 1
